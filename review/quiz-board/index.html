Classroom Quiz Board

A team review game to run from one computer on a projector. Teams pick a topic and a point value; harder questions are worth more.

      Arrow keys move between tiles and Enter opens one. In a question: H shows the hint, R reveals the answer, 1–9 award a team, N is no one, and Esc closes.

        Play again keeps the same teams and deals a new board.
