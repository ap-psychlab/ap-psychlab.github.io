Sky Climb

Climb the floating islands from the forest floor to the treasure at 500 ft. A question waits at each lantern ledge, and the lantern you light becomes your checkpoint. Click the scene, then use ← → (or A / D) to run and Space, W or ↑ to jump: hold for a higher jump, tap for a hop. Hold Shift to walk, and press R to return to your last lantern. On a phone, use the buttons below the scene.
