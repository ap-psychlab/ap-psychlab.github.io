    What's going on?

    Chip has no memory. Every pick is a fresh 70/30 roll, so after any streak the chance of red is still 70%. Believing that blue is “due” after a run of reds is the gambler's fallacy.

    Because red is the more likely pick on every round, the best strategy is to guess red every time (about 70% correct on average). Mixing in blue guesses to match the 70/30 pattern averages only about 58%.
