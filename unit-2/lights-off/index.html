Strategy: two algorithms

An algorithm is a step-by-step procedure that always reaches a solution if you follow it exactly. Both of these work on every puzzle.

Step 1: Chase the lights down

Find the highest row (rows 1 to 4) that still has lights on. Press the cell directly below each of those lights, in any order. Repeat, row by row, until rows 1 to 4 are dark.

Step 2: Fix the bottom row

If lights remain in row 5, press the top-row cells listed by one of the two algorithms below. Then chase the lights down again, and the board is solved.

Algorithm 1: Chase + shortcut Recommended

Look only at columns 1, 2 and 3 of the bottom row. For each one that's on, press the matching top-row cells.
