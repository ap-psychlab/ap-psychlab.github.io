How the algorithm works

An algorithm is a step-by-step procedure that always reaches a solution if you follow it exactly. This one works on every puzzle.

Step 1: Chase the lights down

Find the highest row (rows 1 to 4) that still has lights on. Press the cell directly below each of those lights, in any order. Repeat, row by row, until rows 1 to 4 are dark.

Step 2: Fix the bottom row from the top

If lights remain in row 5, look only at its columns 1, 2 and 3. For each light that's on, press its top-row cells:

If a top-row cell comes up twice, press it twice. Columns are numbered 1 to 5 from the left.

Step 3: Chase again

Chase the lights down once more, and the board is solved.
