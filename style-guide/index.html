  • All buttons: 44px min height, 8px radius (--radius-btn), Lato 700, shrink to 97% when pressed.
  • .btn: --btn fill, --bg text, hover --btn-hover.
  • .btn.alt: --btn2 fill, --heading text, hover darkens 5%.
  • .btn.ghost: transparent, --btn border, hover --bg-alt.
  • Disabled: 50% opacity, no hover or press effect.

Button row

Layout & spacing

  • main: single centered column, max-width 900px, padding 16px top, 20px sides, 24px bottom.
  • main.wide: max-width 1400px, for pages with side-by-side columns. Use it with .split (or a demo's own grid), not for plain text pages.
  • Spacing follows an 8px grid. Bars below show the common steps.
  • Headings have 24px above and 8px below. A heading that's the first thing in its container (page, card, section) has no space above.

Title bar & controls

Drawn by shared/theme.css and wired by shared/ui.js (markup and options are at the top of that file). Every demo page starts with the title bar.

Page templates

  • Column: <header class="topbar"> + <main> (900px). Reading-heavy pages, quizzes, small demos.
  • Split: <header class="topbar wide"> + <main class="wide"> with .split (1400px; 2/3 play area, 1/3 side panel). Below 1000px the panel stacks, so text needed while playing also goes next to the play area.
  • Stage: <header class="topbar wide"> + <main class="wide"> with one area (1400px). Extras open in a modal.
  • Load order: theme.css, style.css, then shared/data/topics.js, shared/data/demos.js, shared/ui.js, script.js (all defer).

Title bar

The band spans the window; its contents line up with main. ui.js adds the ⓘ button (its popover comes from demos.js) and the sound and settings icons. Below 760px the modes take their own row. These examples sit inside this page's column.

Example modal

A native <dialog class="modal">. Focus stays inside, Escape or a click on the backdrop closes it, and focus returns to the button that opened it.

Open with data-modal="id" or APPSYCH_UI.openModal('id'); add class="wide" for a 1000px modal.

Accessibility

:focus-visible
  • Focus outline: 3px --heading, 3px offset. Shows on keyboard focus for buttons, links, and form fields.
  • hidden attribute always hides an element, even one with its own display setting.
  • When a device has "reduce motion" on, button transitions and press-shrink are turned off.
  • Don't rely on color alone: pair colors with a label, icon, or pattern.
