Buttons

Hover, click, and Tab to each to see their states. One primary button per screen.

.btn
.btn.alt
.btn.ghost
.btn disabled
.btn.alt disabled
.btn.ghost disabled
  • All buttons: 44px min height, 8px radius (--radius-btn), Lato 700, shrink to 97% when pressed.
  • .btn: --btn fill, --bg text, hover --btn-hover.
  • .btn.alt: --btn2 fill, --heading text, hover darkens 5%.
  • .btn.ghost: transparent, --btn border, hover --bg-alt.
  • Disabled: 50% opacity, no hover or press effect.

Button row

.row centers and wraps its items with an 8px gap and 16px below (dashed outline shows the row).

Cards

Card title

A card groups related content, like results or instructions. It has a --card background, 1px --border, 12px radius (--radius-card), 16px padding, and a faint --shadow.

Layout & spacing

  • main: single centered column, max-width 900px, padding 16px top, 20px sides, 24px bottom.
  • main.wide: max-width 1400px, for pages with side-by-side columns. Use it with .split (or a demo's own grid), not for plain text pages.
  • Spacing follows an 8px grid. Bars below show the common steps.
  • Headings have 24px above and 8px below. A heading that's the first thing in its container (page, card, section) has no space above.

Accessibility

:focus-visible
  • Focus outline: 3px --heading, 3px offset. Shows on keyboard focus for buttons, links, and form fields.
  • hidden attribute always hides an element, even one with its own display setting.
  • When a device has "reduce motion" on, button transitions and press-shrink are turned off.
  • Don't rely on color alone: pair colors with a label, icon, or pattern.
