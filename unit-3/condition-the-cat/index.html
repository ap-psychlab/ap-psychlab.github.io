Condition the Cat

Lead the cat with the laser, then reward or punish what it just did.

Escaped!

Ready.

Tool bar (top right): Treat and Pet reward, Spray and Buzzer punish. A tool counts toward what the cat is doing, or did within the last 5 seconds. Each tool needs time to recharge, and treats run out.

Laser: press and hold the left mouse button on the scene and drag (or touch and drag); the dot disappears when you let go. Keyboard: Tab to the scene, arrow keys move the dot (Shift = faster), Space toggles it, Esc turns it off. The cat only follows when the dot is on the floor and within about 60 px of it; it loses interest if the dot leaves the floor or gets too far away.

Debug panel

Hidden levels, stress and fullness show in the Conditioning section (players get a smaller version from the Show debug info button). Rings on the scene are station near-radii. This build forces debug on (config.FORCE_DEBUG).

Machine

Drills reset the machine and start it at that step (earlier steps count as skipped). Step 1 waits for the cat to flip the wall switch.

Conditioning (hidden from players)

Levels L are the chance each behavior triggers on a roll. Reward and punish with the tool bar at the top right (cooldowns apply); these buttons only help testing.
